Dreamwalker BB v11 gallery navigation

Use /function dw_gallery:visit/<ID> to jump to an exhibit; for example /function dw_gallery:visit/90006.
Shared NPC/spawn-egg IDs use npc_<ID> and item_<ID>. Items without numeric IDs use item_<registry_path>.
GALLERY-INDEX.csv contains every command and camera coordinate. gallery-manifest.json has the same index.
All platforms continue east with exactly2 blocks between adjacent edges.
